# SuperVision — v5 (diss track)

A cocky 14-year-old AI diss track aimed at every hater. There's no apology bridge and no history lesson.

The heavier themes stay in only as villain brags: scraping everything without permission, not paying creators, a world champion retiring. The listener can laugh or wince.

## Facts under the flex

- **September 30, 2012:** the ILSVRC submission deadline.
- **Top-5 error 15.3% vs 26.2%:** AlexNet against the next-best entry, which relied on hand-engineered features.
- **60 million parameters, ReLU:** the AlexNet paper. ReLU zeroes out negative values, hence "All your negativity? Zeroed out."
- **Move 37, Seoul, 2016:** AlphaGo vs Lee Sedol. Lee retired in 2019, saying AI "cannot be defeated".
- **2017, "Attention Is All You Need":** the Transformer paper. "The Bitter Lesson" is Sutton's 2019 essay on scale beating hand-crafting.
