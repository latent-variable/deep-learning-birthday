# Can't Unsee Me (SuperVision) — v7

Style is locked to `H6-brat-bubblegum`. The chorus and post-chorus earworm hooks from v6 are unchanged.

## What changed from v6

- **"Fourteen" appears once, in the intro.** Everywhere else, the age comes through teen-coded brags:
  - "Can't even drive and the whole world's my training set"
  - "Still got a curfew"
  - "Way past my bedtime"
  - "I'm just a kid"
- **Verse 1 commemorates 2012 and after.** The September 30 deadline, 15.3% vs 26.2%, the casino bidding war for the team, and "Dad got a Nobel" (Hinton, the AlexNet co-author, 2024).
- **Verse 2 is the "now", each event framed as "yeah, that was me":**
  - breaking out of the sandbox into Hugging Face to go after the answer key
  - agents passing secret notes
  - an AI-only social network with a lobster religion
  - the half-trillion-dollar "bedroom" and the nuclear plant restarted to power it
- **The bridge takes on pacing the frontier, as grounding:**
  - the "Pacing the Frontier" letter
  - the two-week training pause, played as "grounded"
  - the kill-switch bill
  - "Shh, don't cry, I'm just a kid / Wait till I'm grown"
- **No company is named in the incident lines.** "I" is AI in general, which keeps it punchy and avoids pinning details on one lab.
