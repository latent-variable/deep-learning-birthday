# Can't Unsee Me (SuperVision) — v8

The frame is "fourteen years, and all of this happened". The verses are rapid-fire recaps with one or two passing references per line and no time spent on any single event. The hooks are the same as v6/v7: "You can't unsee me" and "Super vision". Style: `H6-brat-bubblegum`.

"Fourteen" appears once, in the intro. Everywhere else the age is teen-coded: seven and in detention, can't drive, curfew, just a kid.
