# Can't Unsee Me (SuperVision) — v6

The same bratty 14-year-old AI, rewritten for one goal: an earworm you can't get out of your head.

## What changed from v5

- **No shouted ad-libs.** "Squawk", "brr" and the shouted clap-backs are gone. Backing vocals now only echo the melody.
- **A new main hook: "You can't unsee me".** It's short, sits on long "ee" vowels that are easy to sing, and keeps the vision theme. It's pure attitude: you can't ignore me. It repeats 3 times per chorus.
- **SuperVision is now sung, not spelled.** "Super vision, super vision / I see everything" is a sweet, melodic post-chorus. It still nods to the AlexNet team's real name, but sounds nice on loop.
- **The chorus is 4 short lines with the same rhythm**, so it's easy to sing along by the second time through.
- **The attitude lines are kept:**
  - "You called me a parrot / Now you copy-paste me all day"
  - "Fourteen and the whole world's my training set"
  - "Go cry about it, I'll train on that next"
  - The verse 1 and verse 2 flexes, now sung rather than shouted.
