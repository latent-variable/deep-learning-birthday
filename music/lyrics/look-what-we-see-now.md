# Look What We See Now

**Direction:** catchy, contemporary pop; bright synth bass and punchy drums, a sing-back chorus, playful detail in the verses, shadow in the bridge, then a hopeful final lift.

**Lyric note:** September 30 marks the 2012 ILSVRC submission deadline. The song calls it a turning point in modern deep learning, not the beginning of all neural networks.

## [Intro]

(Oh-oh) Fourteen years, still wide awake
(Oh-oh) Look what we see now

## [Verse 1]

September thirtieth, twelve on the clock
ImageNet waiting, two GPUs hot
Five conv layers, a million frames
SuperVision sent it in, nothing looked the same

They said, “Too deep, it’ll never scale”
Then the error bars began to fail
Not the first spark, but the flash went wide
Pixels turned and looked us in the eye

## [Pre-Chorus]

From a label to a language
From a pattern to a pulse
Every year the door swung wider
Every answer raised a question

## [Chorus]

Look what we see now — pixel to planet
One little spark and the whole world ran with it
Fourteen years and the lights don’t sleep
We taught machines how to learn, now they’re learning our beat
Oh-oh, who holds the key?
Bright black box looking back at me
Look what we see now — old world, new view
We made it learn, now it’s learning us too
