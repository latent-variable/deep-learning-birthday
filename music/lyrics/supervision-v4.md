# SuperVision — v4 (sung by the AI)

Persona: deep learning itself, personified as a bratty, rebellious 14-year-old AI. It was "born" when AlexNet was submitted on September 30, 2012. It knows it's going to run the world and claps back at every hater.

The vocal is female, snappy, all attitude, with a robotic sheen. Catchiness comes first.

## Hooks

- **Clap-back call-and-response.** The lead quotes the hater and the gang shouts the answer: "Too deep!" (Deeper!) / "Can't scale!" (Watch me!) / "Just math!" (Do the math!).
- **Glow-up.** "Gradient descent? That's a glow-up, bet."
- **Training set.** "Fourteen and the whole world's my training set."
- **Spelling chant.** "S-U-P-E-R! (Vision!)". SuperVision was the real name of the AlexNet team.
- **Pre-chorus insult list.** Black box, phase, parrot, ending on: "Guess who you're quoting these days?"

## Real references in character

- **Lineage, so it's not the first neural network.** "Grandpa was a perceptron" (1958), "Backprop in the eighties, then a long, long wait" (the AI winter).
- **AlexNet itself:**
  - born on the September 30 deadline
  - two GPUs
  - 60 million weights, ReLU and dropout ("Dropout's for my layers, I'm still in school")
  - top-5 error of 15.3%
- **Verse 2 growing up:**
  - Move 37 in Seoul
  - "Twenty-seventeen, I finally paid attention / Turns out that's all I needed" ("Attention Is All You Need")
  - diffusion ("I dream in noise")
  - the Bitter Lesson ("tastes sweet")
- **Conscience, in character.** The bridge drops the attitude for four lines. "I'm made of you, every word you wrote… If I'm a mirror, careful what you show me / Raise me right, I'm only fourteen." That covers training data, consent and responsibility without breaking the persona.
