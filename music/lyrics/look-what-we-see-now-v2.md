# Look What We See Now (v2 draft)

Revision of `look-what-we-see-now.md`. The original is unchanged.

## What changed and why

- **Names the birthday.** v1 never says "AlexNet" or "birthday". v2 names both in the intro and outro.
- **One core metaphor: sight.** AlexNet was a vision model, the team was literally called SuperVision, and the hook is "Look what we see now". v2 steers the loose images (beat, key, screen, planet) back toward seeing, dreaming and choosing.
- **A story with people and place.** Verse 1 now moves bedroom, then GPUs, then submission, then "the world didn't know it yet". That gives the video a protagonist and a small-to-huge arc: pixel to planet.
- **Accuracy fixes.**
  - "Twelve on the clock": the deadline was 23:00 GMT, so the line is cut.
  - "A million frames": ImageNet held still images, not video frames.
  - "Error bars began to fail": error bars don't fail, error *rates* fall.
  - "A move no book could fill": vague, replaced.
- **Pre-chorus now rhymes and builds.** v1's pre-chorus had no rhyme and landed on "question". v2 lands on "choice", which sets up the outro "What do we choose now?".
- **Singable chorus line.** "We taught machines how to learn, now they're learning our beat" (14 syllables) becomes "We taught it to see, now it dreams what we dream".
- **"SuperVision" chant.** The team's real name is a pun on the hook. It works as a crowd chant and as an on-screen title card.
- **Model hygiene.** The model-input version (`look-what-we-see-now-v2.acestep.txt`) has no `##` headers and no curly quotes or em-dashes. Acronyms are hyphenated (G-P-Us), and "Lee Se-dol" is split into syllables.
