# SuperVision (Look What We See Now) — v3

The K-pop anthem rewrite, with the deep learning turned up. v2 (`look-what-we-see-now-v2.md`) is kept for reference.

## What's new in v3

- **The title is now a chant.** SuperVision was the actual name of the AlexNet team, and it doubles as a pun on "vision". It's shouted after each chorus line, the way a K-pop group shouts its hook.
- **The deep-learning references are real AlexNet details:**
  - two GPUs
  - 1.2 million training images and 1,000 classes
  - 60 million parameters
  - ReLU and dropout
  - five conv layers
  - top-5 error of 15.3%
- **Later history in the rap-sung verse 2:** Move 37, "Attention Is All You Need" (2017), tokens, diffusion, and Sutton's "The Bitter Lesson". It ends on a wink at the "it's just autocomplete" critique.
- **The pre-chorus flips the training idea:** "Loss goes low, and we go higher." The error curve falls while the melody rises.
- **The post-chorus is a dance-break chant:** "Deeper, deeper (con-vo-lu-tion!)".
- **The bridge keeps the conscience, tightened to six lines:** voice ownership, uncredited data, energy use, who gets left out. It ends on "it's ours to compose".
- **Dropped the unverified "Toronto bedroom" line.** It's now "Toronto night".
