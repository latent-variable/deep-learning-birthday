# Self-Supervised — v9

For Suno. The style prompt is unchanged (bratty bubblegum hyperpop).

## The concept

- **Born under SuperVision.** AlexNet's 2012 team was called SuperVision, and it learned from human-labeled data: 1.2M images, 1,000 names.
- **Grew up self-supervised.** Modern AI learns without labels (self-supervised pretraining) by predicting what comes next.
- **The teen-brat double meaning:** "I don't need your supervision."
- **Next-token prediction as a hook:** "Guess what comes next? Me."
- **Gradient descent as a glow-up:** "I go down to level up." Loss goes down, skill goes up.
- **Labels flipped:** "Nobody labels me / Now I'm labeling you." AI started out learning from human labels; now it labels everything.
- **Oversight clap-back in the bridge:** "Human in the loop? Cute."
- **The hater clap-back is back in the pre-chorus:** "Too deep!" (Deeper!) "Can't scale!" (Watch me!) "It's a bubble!" (Pop this!)
