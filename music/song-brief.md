# Song brief

Working concept: an original celebratory song for the September 30, 2012 AlexNet/ImageNet submission anniversary, framed as the spark that helped ignite modern deep learning.

Keep historical lyrics precise: September 30 was the ILSVRC submission deadline. Preliminary results appeared October 8, with full results October 13. AlexNet's win was a turning point, not the literal beginning of neural networks or deep learning.

## Sound and point of view

- Catchy, current pop with a bright, immediate chorus and a darker, honest bridge
- Big, clear lead vocal; stacked group vocals and short ad-libs in the hook
- Celebrate the field's arc without claiming deep learning began in 2012
- Include AlexNet/ImageNet, GPUs and convolution; AlphaGo's Move 37; the Transformer and its 2017 paper; chat and image-generation culture
- Put skepticism, black-box limits, biased or unconsented data, creative labor, energy use, and responsibility in the lyric
- Target roughly 3 minutes for the first generation, then edit to fit the video
