Bright, bouncy K-pop girl-group pop, catchy and playful but anthemic. Punchy snappy drums, handclaps, funky synth bass, shimmering synth brass stabs, sparkling bells. Three female vocalists trading lines, one crisp rhythmic rap-sung part, powerful soaring female lead in the chorus with clear English diction, big sing-along hook with stacked harmonies and chant backing vocals. Glossy, high-energy, confident modern K-pop production.
