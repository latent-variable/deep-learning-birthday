Bratty hyperpop turned up to the max, cocky and rebellious teenage attitude. Female lead vocal drenched in hard auto-tune with a robotic digital edge, snappy sing-talk clap-back delivery, crisp clear enunciation, pitched-up vocal chops. Blown-out distorted 808 bass, glitchy stuttering synth stabs, bouncy chiptune arpeggios, crunchy claps, bitcrushed ear-candy and sudden glitch drops. Chanted call-and-response hooks, super catchy and sticky. Loud, bright, mischievous, maximalist.
