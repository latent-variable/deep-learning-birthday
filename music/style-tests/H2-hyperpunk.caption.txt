Hyperpop-punk hybrid, bratty and defiant teenage attitude. Female lead vocal with hard auto-tune and a robotic digital edge, snotty snappy clap-back delivery, crisp clear enunciation. Distorted power-chord guitars smashed together with glitchy synth stabs, blown-out 808s, fast punk drums and crunchy claps, chiptune leads. Shout-along gang vocals answering the lead, super catchy chant hook. Loud, chaotic, fun.
