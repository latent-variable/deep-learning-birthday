Bratty hyperpop anthem with a huge chant-along chorus, cocky rebellious teenage attitude. Female lead vocal with glossy hard auto-tune and a robotic edge, snappy clap-back verses, crisp clear enunciation, then a big shouted chorus with stacked gang vocals. Bouncy distorted 808s, glitchy synth stabs, sparkly chiptune arpeggios, huge claps, festival-size drops. Super catchy, triumphant, mischievous.
