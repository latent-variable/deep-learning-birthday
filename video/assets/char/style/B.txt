Abstract geometric character poster, Swiss modernist meets hyperpop, printed as a risograph with fluorescent pink, blue and yellow inks on cream paper, halftone gradients, slight misregistration. The character is a teenage girl made of simple geometric shapes: a circle face with two square pixel eyes and a tiny smirk, two circular cooling-fan hair buns with spiral blades, a neon bounding-box frame drawn around her head with a small tag reading "0.99", a triangle bomber jacket with the number 15.3, stick-thin legs in chunky platform shoes. She strikes a confident idol pose with one hand on hip. Bold negative space, very stylish, graphic design award winning.
